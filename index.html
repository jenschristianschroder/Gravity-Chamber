Course tips & fullscreen

Finish a track to unlock the next. Earn time medals, a clean-run badge, and an all-cells badge. Normal play has frequent checkpoints; Expert checkpoints is an optional challenge with separate records. Demos and section practice do not unlock tracks.

Twists and forward/sideways nudges are optional advanced controls in Settings. Leaning, braking, and upward hops work with either setting. Recenter from the pause menu whenever your grip changes.

Keep hold of your phone. Gentle movements are enough. Checkpoints catch you after a fall, with a two-second time penalty. Progress and each track's best time are saved on this device.

Follow the coral chevrons at each launch ramp and aim for the mint landing ring. Your best ghost and checkpoint differences help you find a faster line. The speaker button switches sound on or off.

Gameplay fills the screen and stays fixed to your phone. On iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen, then launch from the new icon. Sensor readings stay on your device.
