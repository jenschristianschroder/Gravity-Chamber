A SMALL GUIDE

Let your hands
do the thinking.

  1. Start upright. Allow motion access, then hold your phone still with its physical top edge up for a moment. This calibrates which way gravity points.
  2. Follow the amber beacon. Tilt the chamber so the orb falls toward it. The beacons sit at different depths.
  3. Move and turn. Gentle shifts add momentum. Turning the phone changes how the orb moves.
  4. Settle into the glow. Slow the orb inside each beacon briefly to collect it. Reach all six, including the upper corners.

Keep hold of your phone. Small movements are enough. Gameplay needs no touch controls; buttons only start, pause, and restart.

The game stays fixed to your phone as you turn it. For edge-to-edge play on iPhone, use Safari’s Share → Add to Home Screen, then open the game from that icon. The demo uses simulated movement.
