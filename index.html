Course tips & fullscreen

Finish a track to unlock the next. Six tracks get progressively harder with narrower bridges, faster gates, more jumps, and fewer checkpoints. Demos let you preview every course and do not unlock tracks.

Keep hold of your phone. Gentle movements are enough. Checkpoints catch you after a fall, with a two-second time penalty. Progress and each track's best time are saved on this device.

Follow the coral chevrons at each launch ramp. Listen for energy chimes, checkpoint tones, and landing sounds. The speaker button switches sound on or off and remembers your choice.

Gameplay fills the screen and stays fixed to your phone. On iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen, then launch from the new icon. Sensor readings stay on your device.
